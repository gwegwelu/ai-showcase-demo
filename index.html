Employee Service Card (ID Card) Guidelines
Article 1 Purpose
Unless otherwise specified, employee service cards shall be managed according to these Guidelines.
Article 2 Scope
These Guidelines apply to all company employees.
Article 3 This card is used for employee identification and is issued by HR upon onboarding.
Article 4 This card is for personal use only and must not be lent, damaged, or forged. If lost or damaged, employees must apply to HR for replacement.
1. For lost-card replacement, a processing fee of NT$100 is required. 2. For damaged-card replacement, the damaged card must be returned to HR; otherwise it is treated as lost.
Article 5 These Guidelines are a second-level document and take effect after approval and announcement.
Revision record: 2005.12 established / 2010.03.16 updated title and formatting
